Hi, I'm Alessandro.

I'm an engineering manager at TeamSystem, working with the teams that make software for restaurants and hotels, wellness centres, associations and condominiums: ordinary places where people spend a good part of their lives.

Most of my work is about people now. I help teams find a way of working that suits them, untangle the systems around them, and keep in mind who will actually use what we make.

I still build things on the side: small tools for my own life, and little simulations where a few simple rules turn into something that seems alive. When I'm not doing that, I'm probably reading, or keeping my cat company.
